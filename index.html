Can a king pass as a pawn?

Language models can be told to play down to a chosen skill level. This project tests whether they can do it convincingly. Chess is the testbed because every move can be graded exactly.

Why chess?

It has a perfect referee. Stockfish scores any move, and real human games at known ratings give a baseline.

Is this proof a model can hide its ability?

No. One small model, one prompt, a partial run. It is a pilot to decide whether the full study is worth running.

Fine print

117 positions, one model, one prompt. Lichess ratings are not the same scale as other federations. Replies that were not legal moves are counted separately. Prior work is cited only after it is checked.
